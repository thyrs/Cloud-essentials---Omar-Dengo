1.Navigate to Cost Management:1 min.
Log in to the Azure Portal. In the top search bar, type Cost Management + Billing and select it.
2.Select Your Scope:Prerequisite.
Under Cost Management in the left menu, click Budgets. Ensure you have selected the correct Scope (such as a specific Subscription or Resource Group) at the top of the page.
3.Add a New Budget:2 min.
Click the + Add button. Fill in the budget details:
Name: Provide a descriptive name for your budget.
Reset Period: Choose Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.
Creation / Expiration Date: Define when the budget tracking starts and ends.
Amount: Enter your budget limit.
