# Lab_Sign_up_to_AWS.md [x]

Requisites:
* Personal email account
* Debit/Credit Card

### Step 1:
* Go to: www.aws.amazon.com 
### Step 2:
* Select "Create an AWS Console" after clicking on "Sign In to the Console"
### Step 3:
* Create a new Account and fill in all required information, choose a Personal Account
### Step 4:
* Fill in your Credit Card/Debit Card information.
### Step 5:
* Choose Basic Support


### try to create a budget with 10 USD, send SS
* click on your usename at the right upper corner
* click on "billing and cost management"
* at the left menu look for "budgets" click on it
* create new budget > Use a template (simplified) > Monthly cost budget
* create a Budget name: yournames_budget
* Enter your budgeted amount $10
* add an email for notifications
