### Step 1:
* Go to: www.aws.amazon.com 
### Step 2:
* Select "Create an AWS Console" after clicking on "Sign In to the Console"
### Step 3:
* Create a new Account and fill in all required information, choose a Personal Account
### Step 4:
* Fill in your Credit Card/Debit Card information.
### Step 5:
* Choose Basic Support
